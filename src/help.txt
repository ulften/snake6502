SNAKE for the Picocomputer 6502

Steer the snake to the apples. Each apple makes it longer and leaves a
stone behind. Don't hit a wall, a stone or yourself. The snake gets
faster as you go, and a golden apple worth 5 points shows up now and
then for a few seconds. Eat 15 apples to reach the next level, with
new walls and a short snake again.

  A      turn left (relative to the head)
  D      turn right
  ARROWS go up, down, left or right
  P      pause / resume
  SPACE  start
  ESC    end the round (press reset to leave the game)

The high score is saved in snake.hi on the USB drive.
